Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose the right fragrance?

Start with a scent family, intensity, occasion, and budget, then compare samples or travel sizes before committing to a full bottle. Contact us if you need help using the filters.

Who handles returns and refunds?

The retailer that completed the purchase handles returns and refunds under its own policy. Check the offer on the retailer's site or contact that retailer for help with an existing purchase.

Does Fragrance Collect ship internationally?

Shipping is handled by each retailer. International availability, timing, and cost vary by retailer and destination, so confirm the details on the offer page.

Can I get samples before purchasing?

Sample availability depends on the retailer. Check the retailer's listing for sample or travel-size options before purchasing.
